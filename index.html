Behind the journey

A Story of Faith, Hope, and New Beginnings

My name is Steven. For 34 years, alcohol was part of my life. Addiction cost me opportunities, relationships, and stability. There were times I wondered whether I could find peace and purpose again.

But my story isn't finished. I'm choosing recovery, strengthening my faith, and learning to rebuild my life, one day at a time.

I created this space for people who feel alone, discouraged, or uncertain about the future. You are welcome here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is My Recovery Journey a treatment center?

No. This is a personal website, not medical advice, therapy, or an emergency service. For treatment options, visit FindTreatment.gov.

Do I have to be Christian to be here?

No. Faith is part of my story, but everyone seeking recovery or supporting someone is welcome.

Can I attend a meeting just to listen?

Often, yes. Check each meeting's format and attendance requirements; you do not have to share before you're ready.

What if I have a setback?

A setback doesn't erase your progress. Reach out to someone you trust and seek professional support as needed. If you've been drinking heavily and may experience withdrawal, get medical guidance promptly; alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous.
