COMMON QUESTIONS

A little clarity
for the journey.

Do I have to be religious to use this site?

No. Faith is part of my story, but everyone is welcome. Recovery can take many forms.

Are my journal entries public?

No. The gratitude journal and checklist store information locally in your current browser. Clearing browser data may erase it; anyone using the same device and browser may be able to access it.

Does this website provide professional treatment?

No. This is a personal recovery and encouragement project. For clinical advice or treatment, contact a qualified professional.
